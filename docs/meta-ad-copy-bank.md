## Core Offer

Primary CTA: Create free beta account

Landing path: `/beta`

Post-click promise: verify email, set a few study preferences, then choose one starter action.

## Ad Variations

Short:
Nurse Command helps nursing learners turn practice, notes, and uploaded study materials into one focused study workflow. Open beta is available now.

Workflow:
Start with a quick practice set, review missed patterns, upload study material, and come back to one next action. Nurse Command is built for repeated nursing study.

Materials:
Have notes, guides, or study links? Add them to Nurse Command, review the generated tools, and keep cards, quizzes, and study guides together.

Practice:
Build a simple practice loop: answer questions, review rationales, repair weak areas, and track practice evidence in one dashboard.

Beta:
Nurse Command is in open beta for nursing study support. Create a free beta account, verify your email, and test the first study loop.

## Do Not Use

- "Are you failing nursing school?"
- "Struggling with the NCLEX?"
- "We know you are behind."
- "Pass guaranteed."
- "Predict your NCLEX score."
- "Clinical advice" or patient-care claims.
- Any copy that implies knowledge of a viewer's health, grades, stress, identity, finances, or exam status.

## Landing Copy Rules

- Keep "practice evidence only" visible near readiness/adaptive language.
- Mention verification email before account activation.
- Avoid pass-rate, score-prediction, or official NCLEX affiliation claims.
- Put the first action above the fold: Create free beta account.
- Make privacy expectations plain: no PHI uploads.
